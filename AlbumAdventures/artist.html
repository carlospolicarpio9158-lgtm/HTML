The Artist

Jorge Rivera-Herrans

Creator of EPIC the Musical

Jorge Rivera-Herrans is a composer, lyricist, and creative director based in Los Angeles. Merging orchestral brass with heavy pop-electronic production, he crafts cinematic, character-driven concept albums that explore themes of survival, and ancient mythology.

Across the multi-year development of EPIC, Rivera-Herrans has adapted Homer's Odyssey into a nine-part saga, posted raw songwriting breakdowns to millions of online followers, and built a massive independent cast through viral open calls across social media.
