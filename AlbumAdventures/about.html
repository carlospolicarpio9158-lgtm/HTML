The Start

How a college project became a phenomenon

“In the lonely sea between a man and his kingdom, the ruthlessness of gods spread like the song of sirens.”

The development of EPIC: The Musical traces back to Jorge Rivera-Herrans' student days at Notre Dame, where he first began tinkering with a sung-through adaptation of the Odyssey. Coming off years of studying both classical theater and video game scoring, he set out to bridge the gap between ancient storytelling and modern production.

Equipped with a basic keyboard and a digital audio workstation, he tossed out traditional musical theater conventions in favor of leitmotif-driven soundscapes. Rather than relying on standard pit-orchestra tropes, Rivera-Herrans layered cinematic brass over heavy pop-electronic synth beats, giving the sprawling, multi-part concept album its signature hybrid feel.
