The Concept

A mythical odyssey through godly fury, monstrous sacrifice, and the ruthless cost of returning home.

Jorge Rivera-Herrans's musical masterpiece seamlessly blends the timeless story of Homer's Odyssey with the cinematic scale of modern pop-rock scoring. EPIC: The Musical is a powerful reflection of human perseverance and moral conflict, captured across dynamic sagas through soaring vocals, rich orchestration, and unforgettable character motifs.
